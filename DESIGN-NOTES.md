# JSI Janitorial Cleaning — Design Notes

- Locked structural family: `Technical/trades authority`
- Signature module: `Clean-space scope builder`
- Repeating motif: room zones, glass-grid lines, status readouts, and circular wipe marks
- Palette: deep teal, mint, cool glass, warm amber
- Hero image: original AI-generated editorial janitorial image created for this preview

The design presents cleaning as an organized space-planning process without
claiming that JSI offers any particular service. The scope builder helps a
visitor prepare a contact note but does not transmit information.
