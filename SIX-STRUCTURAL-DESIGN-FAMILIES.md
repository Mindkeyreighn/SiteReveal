# SiteReveal — Six Locked Structural Design Families

These are structural systems, not color themes. Future previews should be assigned to the family that best fits the business and then customized within that family.

1. **Field-service editorial**
   - Reference: North Idaho Mobile Mechanics
   - Vertical utility rail, equipment-first photography, dispatch readouts, service manifest, work-order contact panel.

2. **Heritage specialist**
   - Reference: B & R Boat Care Center
   - Established-business storytelling, longevity and trust evidence, specialty-service presentation, seasonal ownership cycle.

3. **Premium appointment/service**
   - Reference: J K Nails
   - Editorial split hero, refined service menu, preparation ritual, concierge-style appointment panel.

4. **Warm neighborhood business**
   - Reference: Woof Pack Pet Spa
   - Friendly organic hero, welcoming location card, irregular service cluster, customer journey, postcard contact experience.

5. **Technical/trades authority**
   - Reference: Reserved for a future business whose brand naturally supports a technical systems presentation
   - Blueprint/grid visual language, system readouts, technical service rows, diagnostic console, seasonal operating cycle.

6. **Landscape-led local service**
   - Reference: Quality Care Landscaping
   - Outdoor-property photography, lawn-first hero, grounded service presentation, seasonal care calendar, local-owner story, direct quote flow.

## Locked rules

- Do not reuse the same section order across all families.
- Do not reduce a family to a palette swap.
- Preserve verified facts and avoid invented claims.
- Use the business’s existing official logo when one is available.
- Every preview remains responsive and retains the SiteReveal preview disclaimer where applicable.
- Each initial preview stays efficient; deeper personalization follows purchase.
