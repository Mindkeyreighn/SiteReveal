# Spokane Site Design Assignments

## Above and Beyond Moving

- Structural family: Warm neighborhood business
- Signature module: Move-Day Planner
- Motif: Soft organic corners and moving-route shapes

## Summit Trucking Logistics LLC

- Structural family: Technical / trades authority
- Signature module: Shipment-Readiness Manifest
- Motif: Dispatch readouts, route rings, and freight-board rows

## Doug's Hauling & Services

- Structural family: Field-service editorial
- Signature module: Load Sorter
- Motif: Utility-card colors, grounded work textures, and load-category markers

The three sites deliberately use different section order, typography, visual
density, color systems, and signature interactions. Shield Transportation is
not included.
