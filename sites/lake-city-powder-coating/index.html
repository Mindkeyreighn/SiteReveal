Not paint. Powder.

Built to look better—and stay that way.

Powder coating uses an electrostatically applied dry finish that is cured to form a strong, uniform surface. For customers, that means a wide range of visual possibilities and a finish designed to resist the chipping and wear ordinary painted metal can face.

Lake City Powder Coating has contributed finishes to local bicycle builds, wheels, automotive projects, and other custom metal work.
