The Philosophy

Made From Remnants

Every fragment left behind on the brother’s bench — a sliver of antler, a piece of bone, an offcut of leather — carries the same story as the work it came from.

Instead of being discarded, each remnant is reclaimed, shaped, and set by hand into a piece of jewelry that exists exactly once. When it’s claimed, it’s gone.
