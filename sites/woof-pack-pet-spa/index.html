Before the appointment

Help us understand your pup.

  1. Share breed, size, age, and coat condition.
  2. Describe the haircut or grooming result you want.
  3. Mention sensitivities, behavior, or handling concerns.
  4. Confirm current vaccination requirements directly with the salon.
After the spa

Keep the fresh feeling going.

  1. Ask about the right brushing routine.
  2. Watch for any skin sensitivity after grooming.
  3. Keep nails maintained between full appointments.
  4. Plan the next visit based on coat needs.
