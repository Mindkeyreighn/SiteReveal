Website Purchase Terms

Effective July 29, 2026

1. The purchase

A $399 SiteReveal purchase covers one specifically selected website preview for one business. The buyer must identify the business and website during Stripe Checkout. Payment does not include a domain, hosting, ongoing maintenance or services listed below as additional work.

2. Included personalization

The purchase includes delivery of the website files, removal of SiteReveal preview messaging, one focused color and typography direction, placement of the buyer’s supplied logo, placement of up to six buyer-supplied photographs, verified updates to the existing business details and services, selected visual accents, and one consolidated revision list containing up to ten reasonable edits.

3. Buyer responsibilities

The buyer must have authority to act for the named business and must supply accurate contact details, business information, logos, photographs and other requested materials. The buyer confirms that they have permission to use all materials they provide. SiteReveal may pause work while required information or approvals remain outstanding.

4. Additional work

Extra pages, major layout changes, a different site concept, advanced forms, booking tools, third-party integrations, extensive copywriting, new custom imagery, domain registration, hosting, launch assistance and ongoing maintenance are not included in the $399 purchase. Additional work requires a separate written scope and price.

5. Ownership and third-party materials

After payment and completion of the included work, the buyer receives the delivered website files for the selected business. SiteReveal retains its own name, administrative system, reusable methods and unrelated templates. Fonts, libraries, photographs and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective licenses.

6. Accuracy and approval

Preview websites may contain publicly sourced information, draft wording or representative imagery. The buyer is responsible for reviewing and approving names, services, prices, claims, contact information, policies and other business content before public launch.

7. Refunds and cancellations

Contact SiteReveal promptly if a purchase was made in error. Refund eligibility is evaluated according to the work already performed, files or digital materials already delivered, Stripe processing limitations and applicable law. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be waived.

8. No performance guarantee

SiteReveal does not guarantee search-engine ranking, customer leads, sales, revenue, uninterrupted third-party hosting or results from using the website.

9. Contact

Questions about a purchase, correction or cancellation can be sent to freesevenluck@gmail.com.
