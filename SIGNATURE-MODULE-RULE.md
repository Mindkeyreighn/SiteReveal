# SiteReveal — Locked Signature Module Rule

Every preview website must include:

1. One of the six locked structural design families.
2. One business-specific signature module that makes sense only for that business or industry.
3. One related visual motif carried through that module.
4. Industry-relevant wording and imagery.
5. Verified business information only.

The signature module must not be reused unchanged on unrelated businesses. It may guide a visitor, help prepare a call, explain a service visually, or show relevant work—without inventing testimonials, results, qualifications, pricing, or guarantees.

## First four applications

- **Wize Guyz Irrigation:** System Symptom Scan with water-coverage rings and blue diagnostic cards.
- **Quality Care Landscaping:** Curb-to-Back-Fence Property Walk with lawn-striping and property-zone blocks.
- **North Idaho Mobile Mechanics:** Field-Service Triage Board with industrial dispatch labeling.
- **J K Nails:** Visual Style Brief with nail shapes, finish swatches, and length choices.

These additions do not replace or alter the locked structural families.
